The Firm

Specialisation, Experience
and Technical Excellence

The firm Bernardi & Bernardi Advogados Associados is among the first legal structures in the country dedicated exclusively to environmental legal management, having followed — and in many cases influenced — the very consolidation of Environmental Law in the national legal system.

Our practice is distinguished by integrated, multilateral work across law, regulation, technical opinions and the operational reality of projects, particularly in complex settings involving licensing, enforcement, conditions and environmental liabilities, with direct and qualified dialogue with regulatory and enforcement authorities.

We are structured to serve ventures subject to intense regulatory control, in partnership with multidisciplinary technical teams, qualified and sized to the strict needs of each case.

“What sets us apart is the combination of private practice in highly complex projects with service inside the environmental administration — which affords a strategic reading of how the State actually decides.” Zilto Bernardi — Founding partner

Practice Areas

Integrated environmental legal advice

I

Environmental Permitting and Regulation

Legal management of highly complex permitting processes, focused on speed and on synergy between developer, environmental agency and technical consultants.

Preliminary, installation and operating licences before IBAMA and state and municipal agencies Management of licence conditions and regulatory risk analysis Environmental due diligence, audits and legal opinions Control of large portfolios of projects at scale
II

Environmental Litigation and Crisis Management

Legal defence and strategic conduct of highly complex matters in the administrative, judicial and institutional spheres, at every level.

Infraction notices, defences and administrative appeals Civil and criminal investigations before federal and state prosecutors Public civil actions of high institutional impact Drafting, negotiation and monitoring of consent decrees (TAC)
III

Carbon, ESG and Energy Transition

Legal structuring of decarbonisation projects and new energy sources — onshore and offshore wind, solar, low-carbon hydrogen, biofuels and natural gas as transition energy — from regulatory design to the negotiation of the instruments.

Carbon Credits, CBIOs, UCS and Green CPR Licensing and enablement of renewable energy projects Environmental asset contracts and green bonds Decarbonisation inventories and corporate targets Prevention of greenwashing risk
IV

Strategic and Institutional Advisory

Strategic conduct of complex environmental matters, integrating legal analysis, multidisciplinary technical support and qualified institutional engagement.

Engagement with permitting authorities at all three levels of government Forward-looking regulatory reading and liability prevention Due diligence in mergers, acquisitions and corporate transactions Legislative monitoring and drafting
