Integrated environmental legal advice

Four practice fronts, broken down into twelve capabilities that work together: regulatory reading anticipates litigation, permitting management prevents liabilities, and institutional engagement sustains both.

I

Environmental Permitting and Regulation

Legal management of highly complex permitting processes, focused on speed and on synergy between developer, environmental agency and technical consultants.

Permitting of complex projectsPreliminary, installation and operating licences before IBAMA and state and municipal agencies; water rights, vegetation clearance and special authorisations.
Management of licence conditions and portfoliosA single obligations matrix, control of deadlines and evidence, and management of large project portfolios at scale.
Due diligence, audits and legal opinionsEnvironmental legal audits, conclusive opinions and environmental liability clauses in contracts and corporate transactions.
II

Environmental Litigation and Crisis Management

Legal defence and strategic conduct of highly complex matters in the administrative, judicial and institutional spheres, at every level.

Administrative litigationInfraction notices, stop-work orders, defences and appeals before IBAMA and state and municipal environmental agencies, at every administrative level.
Public prosecutors and court litigationCivil and criminal investigations before federal and state prosecutors, high-profile public civil actions and negotiation of consent decrees (TAC).
Crisis management and remediationLegal response to environmental incidents, interface with agencies, communities and oversight bodies, environmental insurance, compensation and damage monitoring.
III

Carbon, ESG and Energy Transition

Integrated legal advice for the sustainability market, carbon credits, the energy transition and strategic ESG planning.

Carbon market and green assetsCarbon Credits, CBIOs, UCS, Green CPR and green bonds; structuring of projects and purchase and sale agreements for environmental assets.
Inventories and decarbonisation targetsTechnical and legal support for emissions inventories and compliance with national and international climate regulation.
ESG governance and greenwashingDefinition of targets and indicators, valuation of social and environmental assets and prevention of legal and reputational risk in environmental communication.
Energy transitionLegal structuring of onshore and offshore wind, solar, low-carbon hydrogen, biofuel and natural-gas-as-transition-energy projects — from licensing and regulatory design to contracts and the negotiation of instruments.
IV

Strategic and Institutional Advisory

Strategic conduct of complex environmental matters, integrating legal analysis, multidisciplinary technical support and qualified institutional engagement.

Institutional engagementQualified dialogue with permitting authorities at all three levels of government, stakeholder management and proactive institutional relations.
Forward-looking regulatory readingLegislative monitoring and drafting, anticipation of regulatory change and prevention of liabilities before they materialise.
Transaction structuringEnvironmental due diligence in mergers, acquisitions and corporate transactions, and structuring of new projects and expansions with multidisciplinary support.
